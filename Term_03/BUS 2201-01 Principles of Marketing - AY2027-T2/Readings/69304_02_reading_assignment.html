Unit 2 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What are the primary steps involved in the strategic planning process, and how do they contribute to the overall effectiveness of marketing initiatives?
  2. How does marketing play a key role in shaping the strategic direction of an organization? What specific strategies or tactics are commonly employed to align marketing efforts with broader organizational goals?
  3. What are the essential components of a marketing plan, and how do they contribute to the successful implementation of marketing strategies? How can these components be tailored to suit different industries or business contexts?
  4. What metrics are typically used to measure the performance and effectiveness of marketing initiatives within a marketing plan? How do these metrics inform decision-making and strategic adjustments?
  5. What ethical considerations should be taken into account when developing and implementing marketing strategies? How do ethical considerations impact consumer perceptions, brand reputation, and long-term organizational success?

Prior to completing the reading, review the Assignment Activity for this Unit so you know what to focus on in the reading to support developing the content of the related assignment(s).

Unit 1 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:
  1. What is the purpose of marketing in connecting businesses with consumers, and how does it contribute to organizational success and societal value creation?
  2. How do businesses identify and analyze consumer needs and wants to develop products and services that meet market demand effectively?
  3. What ethical considerations must be considered in marketing practices, and how do these considerations influence consumer perceptions and brand reputation?
  4. What are the essential components of the marketing mix, including product, price, place, and promotion, and how do they work together to create value for customers and achieve business objectives?
  5. How does the broader marketing environment, encompassing factors such as socio-cultural trends, technological advancements, economic conditions, and regulatory frameworks, impact marketing strategies and organizational decision-making?

Prior to completing the reading, review the Discussion Forum for this Unit so you know what to focus on in the reading to support developing the content of the related assignment(s).

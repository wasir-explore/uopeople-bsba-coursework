Unit 2 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. How have International Financial Institutions (IFIs) evolved since their creation?
  2. What roles have they played in shaping the global economy?
  3. What are some key criticisms levied against?
  4. In what ways do the United Nations Millennium Development Goals intersect with the debate about global economic development?

Read

  1. Nordquist, J., & Katz, D. (2024, January 22). The World Bank and International Monetary Fund should do less to achieve more. The Center for Strategic & International Studies. https://www.csis.org/analysis/world-bank-and-international-monetary-fund-should-do-less-achieve-more
        • This text describes the development, purpose, and criticisms of the International Financial Institutions (International Monetary Fund and World Bank).
  2. World Trade Organization. (2023). World Trade Report 2023. https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/booksp_e/wtr23_e/wtr23_ch2_e.pdf
        • This chapter outlines the current state of globalization. While this is the only required chapter the entire World Trade Report 2023 is available here: https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/publications_e/wtr23_e.htm

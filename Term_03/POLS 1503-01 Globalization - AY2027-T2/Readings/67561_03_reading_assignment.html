Unit 1 Reading Assignment

As you go through the readings and watch the videos, consider the following:

  1. What does Globalization mean?
  2. What are the key components of Globalization?
  3. What are the potential benefits and implications of Globalization?
  4. How Globalization impacts nations, economies, cultures, and societies?

Read

  1. Ionica, C., & Stracuzzi, A. (2021). Advanced professional communication. eCampus Pressbooks. Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/llsadvcomm/
      • Read from these specific sections.
        • - Chapter 10.1 Why use sources 
        • - Chapter 10.3 Types of Sources 
        • - Chapter 10.9 Evaluating Sources 
          • These sections of the chapter give a comprehensive understanding of the significance of sources, and how to identify and evaluate reliable and credible sources of information.  
  2. Long, L., Minervini, A., & Gladd, J. (2021). Write what matters. MSL Academic Endeavors. Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. https://idaho.pressbooks.pub/write/ 
        • Read Section 101. Considering Multiple Views & Avoiding Bias.This section focuses on how bias and personal views influence writing. 
  3. Coccia, M. (2019). World-System Theory: A sociopolitical approach to explain world economic development in a capitalistic economy. Journal of Economics and Political Economy, 5(4), 459-465. https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3315556
      • Open the PDF on the website to read the full article.This article presents an overview of world-system theory and its evolution as it traces the global economy.
  4. Goldberg, P., Reed, T., Milesi-Ferretti, G. M, & Stock, J. H. (Hosts). (2023, May 11). What is the evidence for deglobalization? [Audio podcast episode]. In Brookings Podcast on Economic Activity. Brookings. https://www.brookings.edu/articles/what-is-the-evidence-for-deglobalization/
      • This podcast explores the current state of globalization, its impact on the Covid-19 pandemic, and policy impact on globalization. As you listen to it, or read the transcript available on the website, reflect on the benefits and disadvantages of globalization.
  5. Council on Foreign Relations. (2021, September 1). What is globalization [Video]. CFR Education. Licensed under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. https://education.cfr.org/learn/video/what-globalization 
      • This video offers examples to better understand the benefits and challenges of Globalization.
  6. Vanham, P. (2019, January 17). A brief history of globalization. World Economic Forum. https://www.weforum.org/stories/2019/01/how-globalization-4-0-fits-into-the-history-of-globalization/ 
      • This text delves into the constraints of globalization and technology on culture.
  7. Alkharafi, N., & Alsabah, M. (2025). Globalization: An Overview of Its Main Characteristics and Types, and an Exploration of Its Impacts on Individuals, Firms, and Nations. Economies, 13(4), 91. https://doi.org/10.3390/economies13040091
      • This article covers globalization’s main dimensions (economic, social, cultural, and environmental) and provides real-world examples across different regions.

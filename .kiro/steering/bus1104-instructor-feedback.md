## Standing rules (apply to every BUS 1104 assignment and discussion)

1. **Name real companies and events, not only data and policy.** The instructor wants
   concrete, named examples next to the statistics. Before presenting a draft, check that it
   contains at least a few named firms, institutions, or dated events tied to the argument.
   Statistics, World Bank projects, and central-bank decisions do not count as "named firms."
2. **Cite every named example.** Each firm or event needs a verifiable source and a matching
   reference entry (see Section 1 of `assignment-writing-rules.md`). Do not add an example
   from memory without checking it.
3. **Add examples without breaking the word limit.** Check the assignment's word range first,
   and trim weaker sentences to make room instead of exceeding it.
4. Keep the habits that earned praise: precise figures with a cited source, an honest
   "relative, not absolute" reading of the data, drivers linked to named theory, and caveats
   stated next to the claims they limit.
